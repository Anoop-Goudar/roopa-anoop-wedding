Travel & Stay

ಪ್ರಯಾಣ ಮತ್ತು ವಾಸ್ತವ್ಯ

Everything you need to know to make your time in Bengaluru effortless.

Getting Here

Fly into Kempegowda International Airport (BLR), ~35 km from the venue. App-based cabs (Uber, Ola, Rapido) and airport taxis are readily available. The metro and BMTC AC buses connect the city well.

Where to Stay

We recommend hotels around Rajajinagar, Malleshwaram, MG Road and Indiranagar — comfortable, central, and a short drive to the venue. A curated list of options will be shared closer to the date.

Local Tips

Taste Karnataka while you're here — Vidyarthi Bhavan for Masala Dosa, MTR and CTR for filter coffee and rava idli, Brahmin's Coffee Bar for vada-chutney, and a quiet walk through Cubbon Park or Lalbagh. July evenings can be breezy — carry a light shawl.
